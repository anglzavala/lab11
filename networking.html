Networking basics

Key ideas

Networks rely on devices, protocols, and addresses to move data securely and efficiently.

  • LAN connects nearby devices.
  • WAN connects networks across larger distances.
  • Switches manage local traffic.
  • Routers move traffic between networks.

Network Packet Visualization

Packets are small segments of data that travel through the network and are reassembled at their destination.

Computer Switch Router

What is happening?

A source device creates data and divides it into packets. The switch handles local delivery, and the router forwards traffic when packets must cross networks.
